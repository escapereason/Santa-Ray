## METHODOLOGY & CONSTRUCTION DETAILS

### Excavation Method: Traditional Open Trench

**Why Open Trench (vs. Trenchless):**
- Most cost-effective for 50ft residential laterals
- Direct visual inspection of connections possible
- Easier to ensure proper slope and alignment
- Industry standard for this length and property type

**Trenchless Alternative:**
- Would reduce excavation time by 50%
- Would increase equipment costs by $2,000-3,000
- Total cost: $8,900-9,900 (not cost-effective for 50ft)
- Best for longer runs (>100ft) or difficult access

### Trench Specifications

| Specification | Value | Code Requirement |
|---------------|-------|------------------|
| **Depth** | 4-6 feet | Below frost line, proper drainage slope |
| **Width** | 2-3 feet | Adequate for worker access and compaction |
| **Length** | 50 feet | Building cleanout to city main |
| **Slope** | Minimum 1/4" per foot | Required for gravity flow |
| **Bedding** | 6" sand base | Proper pipe support and drainage |

### HDPE Pipe Specifications

**Material:** High-Density Polyethylene (HDPE)
**Diameter:** 4 inches (standard residential)
**Type:** DR 9 or SDR 35 (pressure-rated for sewer use)
**Color:** Typically black with co-extruded stripes

**Why HDPE:**
- ✅ Corrosion-resistant (unlike cast iron or clay)
- ✅ Flexible (handles ground movement)
- ✅ Long lifespan (50+ years expected)
- ✅ Root-resistant (smooth interior)
- ✅ Meets EBMUD specifications

## EBMUD COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS

### EBMUD Regional PSL Ordinance

**Requirement:** Properties in EBMUD service area must have compliant private sewer lateral before property transfer.

**Compliance Options:**
1. **Replacement** (this project) - Full replacement with modern HDPE piping
2. **CCTV Inspection** - If existing lateral passes video inspection test
3. **Point Repairs** - If only specific sections need repair

**This Property:** Replacement chosen (likely due to age/condition of existing lateral)

### Testing Requirements

**EBMUD Witnessed Test:**
- Pressure test OR video inspection
- Must be witnessed by EBMUD inspector or authorized agent
- Testing adds 2-3 hours to project timeline
- Cannot backfill until test passes

**Test Methods:**
1. **Pressure Test:** Air or water pressure test of sealed system
2. **Video Inspection:** CCTV camera inspection of full length

**Pass Criteria:**
- No leaks detected
- Proper slope verified
- No obstructions or defects
- Full connection to city main confirmed

### Certification Process

1. **Test Completion:** EBMUD inspector witnesses successful test
2. **Documentation:** Photos, test results, as-built documentation
3. **Certificate Issuance:** EBMUD issues compliance certificate
4. **Submit to City:** Certificate required for property transfer approval
5. **Timeline:** Certificate typically issued within 3-5 business days after test

## SAFETY & REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

### Trench Safety Requirements

**OSHA Regulations:**
- Trenches >5 feet deep require protective systems
- Shoring, shielding, or sloping required
- Daily safety inspections mandatory
- Competent person must be on-site

**This Project:** 4-6 foot trench depth = OSHA regulations apply

### Worker Safety Measures

- Hard hats, safety glasses, steel-toe boots (required)
- Trench box or sloping if depth >5 feet
- Ladder access every 25 feet
- Air monitoring if confined space concerns
- First aid kit and emergency plan on-site

### Environmental Compliance

**Soil Disposal:**
- Cannot dump excavated soil anywhere
- Must dispose at approved facility if contaminated
- Testing may be required for old urban soils

**Erosion Control:**
- Sediment barriers if near storm drains
- Daily cleanup to prevent runoff
- Compliance with Oakland stormwater regulations
