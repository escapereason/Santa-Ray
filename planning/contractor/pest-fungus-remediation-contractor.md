#### 2. **Window Sash Work Complexity - +30-90 hours**

**Original Estimate:** 90 hours total for 15 windows (6 hrs per window)
- Main unit windows (8): 48 hours
- Exterior windows (7): 42 hours

**Industry Research Finding:** Historic window restoration on 115-year-old buildings requires **8-15 hours per window**

**Complexity Factors:**
- **Non-standard sizes:** Nothing is standard dimension - requires custom fitting
- **Fungus-damaged sills:** Sills require structural repair before sash installation
- **Hardware preservation:** Attempt to reuse existing hardware (time-consuming)
- **Interior trim minimization:** Careful work to avoid damaging 115-year-old trim
- **Paint prep:** Prime painting of new sashes before installation

**Revised Estimate:**
- Low complexity (pre-built sashes, good frames): 8 hrs/window × 15 = 120 hours
- Moderate complexity (some custom fitting, sill repair): 10 hrs/window × 15 = 150 hours
- High complexity (extensive custom work): 12 hrs/window × 15 = 180 hours

**Recommended:** Use 10 hrs/window average = **150 hours** (vs. 90 hours original)

**Variance:** +60 hours (+67% increase from window estimate)

#### 4. **115-Year-Old Building Complexity Multipliers**

**Research Finding:** Industry standards apply the following time multipliers to old house work:

| Complexity Factor | Multiplier | Reasoning |
|------------------|------------|-----------|
| Non-standard dimensions | 1.15-1.30x | Nothing is square, level, or plumb - everything requires custom fitting |
| Hidden damage discovery | 1.15-1.25x | Buffer for unexpected rot, termite damage, or structural issues found during demo |
| Material preservation | 1.20-1.40x | Careful vs. aggressive demolition to preserve surrounding historic elements |
| Working around old systems | 1.10-1.20x | Knob-and-tube wiring, galvanized plumbing, asbestos materials require care |

**Overall Impact:** 20-35% time increase over new construction for similar work

**Application to This Project:**
- Base estimate assumes some complexity already factored in
- Adding 10-15% buffer for additional discoveries: 528 × 1.10 = 581 hours
- This partially accounts for the gap between 528 and 700 hours

## TIM-BOR TREATMENT REQUIREMENTS

**Application Requirement:** Must be applied by licensed pest control firm
