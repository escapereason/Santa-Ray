## TIMELINE & DEPENDENCIES

### Estimated Timeline: 2-3 Weeks

#### Week 1: Demolition, Remediation, Structural
- **Days 1-2**: Deck disassembly, siding/subfloor removal (Phase 1)
- **Days 3-4**: Mold treatment and containment (Phase 2)
- **Days 5-7**: Structural repairs with temporary shoring (Phase 3)

#### Week 2: Subfloor, Siding, Deck
- **Day 8**: Subfloor installation and waterproofing (Phase 4)
- **Days 9-11**: Siding sheathing and stucco scratch coat (Phase 5 start)
- **Days 12-14**: Stucco brown and finish coats with cure time (Phase 5 complete)

#### Week 3: Reassembly and Finishing
- **Days 15-16**: Deck reassembly and board replacement (Phase 6)
- **Days 17-18**: Painting and final waterproofing (Phase 7)
- **Day 19**: Final inspection and cleanup

### Critical Dependencies
1. **Weather**: Exterior stucco work requires dry conditions (no rain for 48 hours after application)
2. **Cure Times**: Cannot rush stucco curing - minimum 24 hours between coats
3. **Material Delivery**: Lumber and stucco materials must be on-site before starting
4. **Temporary Shoring**: Must remain in place until all structural work complete
5. **Mold Treatment**: Must fully dry before structural work begins

### Gate Checks
- ✅ After Phase 1: Document full extent of damage before proceeding
- ✅ After Phase 2: Verify mold treatment successful (visual inspection)
- ✅ After Phase 3: Structural integrity check before subfloor installation
- ✅ After Phase 5: Stucco cure verification before deck reassembly
- ✅ After Phase 7: Final waterproofing inspection

## NOTES & CONSIDERATIONS

### Key Assumptions
This estimate is based on:
- Experienced but somewhat rusty skill level
- 115-year-old construction with typical complexities
- Traditional 3-coat stucco system (not EIFS synthetic)
- Mold is surface-level requiring treatment (not black mold requiring certified remediation)
- Deck section is approximately 8'x8' (64 sq ft)
- 10-15% material waste factors included
- Weather delays not included in timeline

### Risk Factors
- **Hidden damage**: Additional structural issues may be discovered during demolition
- **Weather delays**: Rain can extend stucco timeline significantly
- **Material availability**: Lumber prices and availability fluctuate
- **Complexity**: Old construction often has surprises

### Recommendations
1. **Start immediately**: Mold should not sit untreated
2. **Address moisture source**: Critical to prevent recurrence
3. **Consider professional mold test**: If extent is uncertain
4. **Document everything**: For insurance and future reference
5. **Plan for contingency**: Budget and timeline may need adjustment
