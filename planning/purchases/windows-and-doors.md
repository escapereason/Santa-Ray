## Windowsills

I will make the windowsills myself out of **White Oak**. They are 3/4" x 3.5" blocks with a small cut out.

Common wood types for a 115-year-old home in Oakland, CA are:
*   Redwood (most likely)
*   Douglas Fir
*   White Oak (less common, for higher-end homes)
